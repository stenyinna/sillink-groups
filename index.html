About Sillink Group

A broad business outlook, grounded in shared progress.

Sillink Group is a diversified business group bringing together complementary sectors under one identity. Our portfolio spans telecommunications, energy, transport, real estate, media, contracting, manufacturing, equipment and financial activities.

We believe that strong businesses are built through dependable execution, clear relationships and the ability to recognise opportunities across industries. Our aim is to connect capabilities and help create value for customers, partners and communities.
